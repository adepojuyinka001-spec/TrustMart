# TRUSTMART SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH (SSOT) v2.0

Version: 2.0
Status: Current / Authoritative
Date: 22 August 2026
Company: TrustMart Limited
Brand: TrustMart
Master brand line: Securing Transactions. Building Trust.

## 1. Master definition
TrustMart is a technology-driven Trust & Commerce Network connecting demand with supply and providing optional transaction-assurance infrastructure that helps buyers and sellers complete eligible transactions with greater confidence.

Two sibling products:
1. TrustMart Marketplace
2. TrustMart Escrow / Transaction Assurance

Both share one identity, business, verification, payment, risk, reward, notification, analytics and audit foundation.

## 2. Marketplace
Marketplace captures:
- structured buyer intent;
- seller inventory;
- intelligent matching;
- interests and leads;
- seller subscriptions;
- listing lifecycle and re-engagement;
- Marketplace analytics.

Marketplace does not require parties to use Escrow.

Seller subscriptions monetize Marketplace access/opportunities. Contact access also requires buyer consent/privacy and valid entitlement.

TrustMart recommends Escrow after parties connect and offers an interactive demo/CTA.

## 3. Escrow
Escrow is a standalone product available for deals originating anywhere, including outside TrustMart Marketplace.

Marketplace-originated Escrow reuses the same Escrow Engine.

## 4. Escrow fee
Current contemplated platform fee: 2.5%, configurable.

Fee responsibility is versioned transaction terms and may be:
- buyer pays;
- seller pays;
- shared by both according to agreed allocation.

Final live funds-flow is subject to approved legal/provider structure.

## 5. Confidential internal reward policy
Current internal model:
- Referral: 0.3%
- Buyer appreciation: 0.1%
- Seller appreciation: 0.1%

Exact percentages are confidential internal information and visible only to authorized staff.

Customer/referrer experiences show reward amount/status, not rate/formula.

No valid external referral => Company Referral.

## 6. Marketplace default settings
Initial configurable defaults:
- qualifying match threshold: 70%;
- listing lifecycle: 14 days;
- weekly seller subscription: ₦5,000;
- monthly seller subscription: ₦15,000.

These values must not be hard-coded.

## 7. Matching
Hard requirements filter first.
Weighted category-specific scoring follows.
Thresholds and weights are configurable.
AI may explain a result but is not authoritative for scoring.

## 8. Rollout
Demand-first and concentrated:
1. Real Estate
2. Vehicles
3. Travel / selected high-value services
4. Import/export and trade
5. broader legitimate categories

## 9. Reviews
After eligible completed Escrow:
- request service feedback from buyer and seller;
- in-app + email + approved/consented WhatsApp;
- rating + written review;
- configurable timing/reminders;
- low ratings can trigger service recovery.

## 11. Non-negotiable safety
- no screenshot-based funding;
- provider verification required;
- ledger is financial truth;
- no silent balance editing;
- Escrow state is backend-authoritative;
- maker-checker for material financial actions;
- AI cannot move funds;
- n8n cannot own financial truth;
- live customer-funds model requires qualified legal/provider approval.

## 12. North Star

Successful Buyer-Seller Matches Resulting in Verified Commercial Progress.

Longer-term:
Successfully Completed TrustMart Transactions.
